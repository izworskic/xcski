# Wisconsin differentiation contract

Wisconsin pages must not reuse Michigan titles, descriptions, regional names, trail blurbs, source copy, or keyword blocks. The product emphasizes Wisconsin's volunteer-reported Cross Country Conditions ecosystem, Wisconsin DNR park/forest trail rules, Birkie-country decisions, Northwoods reliability, and southern-Wisconsin short snow windows.

Every indexed trail page must have a Wisconsin-specific canonical URL, trail description, status-source provenance, nearby Wisconsin alternatives, and region links. The shared XC engine may be identical code.
