# Minnesota differentiation contract

Minnesota pages must not reuse Michigan or Wisconsin titles, descriptions, regional names, trail blurbs, source copy, or keyword blocks. The product emphasizes the Great Minnesota Ski Pass, Minnesota DNR snow-depth/groomed-condition resources, Arrowhead/North Shore snow reliability, Twin Cities snowmaking and metro trail-pass decisions.

Every indexed trail page must have a Minnesota-specific canonical URL, trail description, pass context, status-source provenance, nearby Minnesota alternatives, and region links. The shared XC engine may be identical code.
